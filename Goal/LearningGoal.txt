Very short:

You’re trying to learn how to design and build a real-world multi-tenant Hotel Management SaaS using microservices.

Mainly learning:

🏢 Multi-tenancy & tenant isolation
🧩 Bounded contexts & microservice boundaries
🔄 Service-to-service communication
🗄️ Database-per-service & data ownership
🔐 Custom .NET 10 auth (ASP.NET Core Identity + JWT) + Kong + authorization
⚡ Concurrency & preventing double bookings
🔁 Transactions, idempotency & Saga
📨 RabbitMQ, events, retries & DLQ
🚀 Redis caching & performance
💳 Payment workflows & failures
📊 Eventual consistency & reporting
🛡️ Security, failures & resilience
🤖 Applied ML in a multi-tenant system (cold start, leakage, suggestion-only)

In one line:

Build a real hotel SaaS while learning the actual problems and solutions involved in production-grade microservice architecture, not just theory.
